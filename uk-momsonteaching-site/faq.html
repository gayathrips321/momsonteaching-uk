Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our programmes, pricing, scheduling, and safety.

Fast Answers, No Jargon

About Our Tutors

How are your tutors vetted?

Every tutor goes through an interview and reference check for subject expertise and teaching experience before being matched with a family.

Programmes

Do you offer 11+ preparation for grammar school entrance?

Yes, we offer dedicated 11+ preparation covering Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English, and Maths, tailored to the specific test format used by your target grammar schools.

Can classes be conducted in Hindi, Gujarati, or Punjabi alongside English?

Yes, our mother-teachers can explain concepts in Hindi, Gujarati, Punjabi, or Tamil alongside English instruction, especially for younger students.

Scheduling & Logistics

What happens if we move to a different city?

Tuition continues uninterrupted — our online format means your child keeps the same tutor and schedule regardless of which UK city you move to.

How do you track and report a child's academic progress?

Parents receive progress updates after each session and a detailed monthly report covering topics covered, strengths, and areas for improvement.

Pricing & Payments

What is the fee structure?

We offer both monthly and term-based plans, priced by programme and year group. Full pricing is available on our Pricing page.
