Frequently Asked Questions

What age should my child start 11+ preparation?

Most families begin 11+ preparation in Year 4 or early Year 5, giving 12-18 months to build up to exam standard before sitting in Year 6.

Can the same tutor take my child from GCSE into A-Level?

Where possible, yes — many families prefer continuity, and we try to keep the same subject specialist across both stages where scheduling allows.

Do you offer Indian curriculum tuition alongside UK programmes?

Yes, our Indian Curriculum programme covers CBSE, ICSE, and IGCSE for families keeping their child on the Indian academic track, often alongside UK schooling.
