ABOUT MOMS ON TEACHING UK

Where Education Meets Motherly Care

Moms on Teaching UK is a personalized one-on-one online tutoring platform connecting families across the UK with experienced, verified Teacher-Moms who nurture confidence, curiosity, and academic success.

A Global Community Built Around Every Child

Moms on Teaching began with a simple belief: every child deserves personalized guidance from educators who combine professional expertise with genuine care.

What started as a successful tutoring initiative serving families across the Gulf has now expanded to the UK, supporting families across the UK who seek the best of both educational worlds. Whether your child is following CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, IB, or a UK curriculum, we provide learning that adapts to their unique goals.

Our Mission

To provide every child with personalized one-on-one learning while creating meaningful work-from-home opportunities for qualified Teacher-Moms around the world.

Our Vision

To build a global learning community where children thrive academically and educators empower families through compassionate, personalized teaching.

About Moms on Teaching UK

Moms on Teaching UK is an online 1-on-1 tutoring service for British Indian families, covering 11+ exam preparation, GCSE, A-Level, and Indian Curriculum (CBSE/ICSE/IGCSE) tuition, delivered by qualified mother-teachers.

100%

Real Mother-Teachers

We built this as an extension of Moms on Teaching's work supporting Indian diaspora families internationally, adapted for the specific pressures British Indian families know well — the intensity of grammar school entrance at ten or eleven, and the high stakes of GCSEs and A-Levels that follow.

Why Only Mothers?

Patience

Mothers know how to stay calm through a child's frustration — a skill built from parenting, not just teaching.

Real Teaching Experience

Every tutor is a qualified mother with genuine classroom or tutoring experience in the UK curriculum.

Carefully Vetted

Every tutor goes through an interview and reference check before being matched with a family.

Our Vetting Process

  • ✓ Verified teaching or tutoring background (minimum 3 years)
  • ✓ Subject-matter interview per programme (11+, GCSE, or A-Level)
  • ✓ Interview and reference checks for every tutor
  • ✓ Trial session reviewed by our academic team
  • ✓ Ongoing performance check-ins each term
