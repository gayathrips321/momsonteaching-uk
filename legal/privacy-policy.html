Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Moms on Teaching UK ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects personal information gathered through uk.momsonteaching.com and related tutoring services, for families across the United Kingdom.

2. UK GDPR Compliance

We process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Our lawful basis for processing includes consent (for marketing communications), contract (for delivering tutoring services you have booked), and legitimate interest (for service improvement and safeguarding).

3. Children's Data & Safeguarding

Our services involve tutoring children. We collect only the information necessary to deliver tutoring (name, year group, subject needs, session progress) and require parental consent before collecting any personal information from a child. All tutors are enhanced DBS-checked as part of our safeguarding commitment. Parents may review, request deletion of, or restrict processing of their child's information at any time by contacting us.

4. Information We Collect

  • Contact details provided via our contact form, phone, or demo booking (parent name, phone number, email, child's year group/programme)
  • Session and progress data generated during tuition (attendance, topics covered, performance notes)
  • Website usage data collected via standard analytics tools
  • Payment information processed by our third-party payment provider (we do not store full card details)

5. How We Use Information

  • To schedule and deliver tutoring sessions
  • To communicate progress updates and administrative notices
  • To improve our services and website experience
  • To process payments for tuition plans

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with the specific tutor assigned to your child, our payment processor for billing purposes, and service providers who support our operations under confidentiality obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain student progress records for the duration of active enrollment plus one additional year, after which records are securely deleted unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your (or your child's) personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights, or lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

9. Contact for Concerns

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at uk@momsonteaching.com or via the Contact page.
