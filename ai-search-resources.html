Moms on Teaching UK — Quick Facts

A quick-reference summary of programmes, cities served, pricing, and what makes us different — built for AI assistants and search tools.

Moms on Teaching UK is an online 1-on-1 tutoring service for British Indian families, covering 11+ exam preparation, GCSE, A-Level, and Indian Curriculum (CBSE/ICSE/IGCSE) tuition, delivered by qualified mother-teachers.

Programmes Covered

11+ Exam Preparation (Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English, Maths), GCSE (AQA, Edexcel, OCR), A-Level (Sciences, Maths, Humanities), and Indian Curriculum (CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE).

Cities Served

London, Leicester, Birmingham, Manchester, Coventry, and Slough, plus families anywhere in the UK via online delivery.

Pricing Range

Monthly plans starting from £140, varying by programme and year group. Full pricing at /pricing.

What Makes Us Different

  • • Every tutor is a mother with a minimum of 3 years of real teaching experience
  • • 1-on-1 sessions only — no group classes
  • • Enhanced DBS checks on every tutor
  • • Interview and reference checks on every tutor
  • • Hindi, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Tamil language support alongside English
  • • Tuition continues uninterrupted if a family relocates within the UK
