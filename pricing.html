Pricing FAQs

Can I change plans mid-term?

Yes, you can upgrade or adjust your weekly session count. Changes take effect from the next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major debit and credit cards and bank transfer. Payment links are sent by email each billing cycle.

Is there a registration fee?

There is no separate registration fee for booking a free demo. Any confirmed programme charges will be explained before enrolment.
