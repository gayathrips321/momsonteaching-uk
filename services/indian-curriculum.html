Indian Curriculum Tuition in the UK

Moms on Teaching offers 1-on-1 CBSE, ICSE, and IGCSE tuition for British Indian families across the UK, delivered by verified mother-teachers who understand what it means to keep a child connected to the Indian academic track from abroad.

Quick Facts — Indian Curriculum

  • ✓ Boards: CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE
  • ✓ Grades: KG through 12
  • ✓ Format: 1-on-1 live online classes
  • ✓ Languages: English, Hindi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil

Why Families Choose This Track

  • ✓ Smooths the transition for families who have recently relocated from India
  • ✓ Keeps the door open for NEET/JEE or other India-based entrance exams later
  • ✓ Maintains the rigorous maths and science foundation Indian curricula are known for
  • ✓ Supports families running Indian curriculum in parallel with UK schooling

Programmes Available

CBSE

Grades 1–12, full subject coverage, exam-pattern-matched practice.

ICSE

IGCSE

Cambridge/Edexcel International curriculum, commonly followed by Indian schools abroad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why would a family in the UK keep their child on CBSE or ICSE?

Common reasons include recently relocating from India on a skilled worker visa mid-year, keeping the door open for NEET or JEE entrance exams, or wanting the strong maths and science foundation Indian curricula are known for.

Can my child study on Indian curriculum while also attending a UK school?

Yes, many families run both in parallel — Indian curriculum tuition in the evenings or weekends alongside regular UK school attendance.

Is IGCSE the same as GCSE?

No. IGCSE is the Cambridge International or Edexcel International version of GCSE, commonly followed by Indian schools abroad, and differs in syllabus content and assessment style from the UK's domestic GCSE.
