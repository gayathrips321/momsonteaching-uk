GCSE Tuition for British Indian Families

Moms on Teaching offers 1-on-1 GCSE tuition for British Indian families across the UK, covering core and elective subjects aligned to AQA, Edexcel, and OCR, delivered by verified mother-teachers who understand the pressure of GCSE years.

Quick Facts — GCSE Tuition

  • ✓ Subjects: Maths, Sciences, English, Humanities + electives
  • ✓ Exam boards: AQA, Edexcel, OCR
  • ✓ Format: 1-on-1 live online classes
  • ✓ Years: 9 through 11

Why Families Choose This Programme

  • ✓ Structured lesson plans aligned to your child's specific exam board
  • ✓ Past-paper practice under timed, exam-realistic conditions
  • ✓ Subject specialists assigned by subject, not one generalist tutor covering everything
  • ✓ Regular progress reports so you always know where your child stands

Frequently Asked Questions

Which exam boards do you cover for GCSE?

We cover AQA, Edexcel, and OCR, the three most widely used GCSE exam boards in England, and can advise on Scottish equivalents where relevant.

Can my child combine subjects with different tutors?

Yes, most families work with a small team of subject specialists rather than a single generalist tutor, coordinated by our academic team.

How far in advance should we start GCSE tuition?

Starting at the beginning of Year 9 or 10 gives the most benefit, though we regularly support students starting later, including focused revision support in the months before exams.
