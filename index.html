How a class works

A lesson built around one child.

One-to-one online learning gives the teacher room to listen, teach, practise and respond to what your child actually needs.

01

Understand

We start with your child's year group, curriculum, subject and learning goals.

02

Teach

The teacher explains concepts at the right pace, with examples that make sense to the learner.

03

Practise

Your child applies the idea through guided questions, discussion and purposeful practice.

04

Build confidence

Each lesson creates a clearer next step, so progress feels manageable and measurable.
