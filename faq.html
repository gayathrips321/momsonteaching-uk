Getting Started

How does the free demo class work?
We first understand your child's year group, goals and curriculum. You then meet a suitable tutor in a no-obligation online demo class.
How do I choose the right programme for my child?
We consider your child's year group, current level, school curriculum, upcoming examinations and learning goals before recommending a suitable programme.
Are lessons online or face-to-face?
Our tuition is delivered online in a focused one-to-one format, allowing families across the UK to access suitable tutors without travel.

Academic support

Programmes & Curriculum

Do you offer 11+ preparation for grammar school entrance?
Yes. Our 11+ preparation covers Verbal Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning, English and Maths, tailored to the test format used by the target grammar schools.
Do you support different UK exam boards?
Yes. Tuition can be aligned with the relevant school and exam-board requirements, including AQA, Edexcel and OCR.
Can my child continue the Indian curriculum alongside UK school?
Yes. We offer CBSE, ICSE and IGCSE support for families who want continuity with the Indian academic track.
Can classes be explained in an Indian language?
Yes. Where suitable, concepts can be explained in Hindi, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil alongside English instruction.

Trust & support

Tutors, Safety & Progress

Are your tutors DBS-checked?
Every tutor completes identity verification and the required safeguarding checks before being matched with a family.
How do you track and report academic progress?
Parents receive session updates and regular progress feedback covering topics completed, strengths, gaps and next steps.
Will my child have the same tutor each week?
We aim to provide continuity with a suitable tutor so that your child can build confidence, trust and steady academic progress.

Practical details

Scheduling & Payments

What is the fee structure?
We offer monthly and term-based plans, priced according to the programme, year group and learning requirements. Full details are available on the Pricing page.
What happens if my child misses a class?
Please inform the tutor or support team as early as possible. Any rearrangement or make-up lesson is handled according to the agreed programme terms.
What happens if we move to another UK city?
Because lessons are online, tuition can continue with the same tutor and schedule even if your family moves to another UK city.
